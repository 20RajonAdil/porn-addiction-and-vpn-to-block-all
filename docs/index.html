MAAR Shield: Privacy Policy

MAAR Shield is a self-control app that blocks pornography and 18+ websites on your own device.

What it does

  • VPN service: creates a local VPN on your device only to filter DNS lookups. It does not route or inspect your traffic, and no data is sent to us.
  • Accessibility service: used only to detect when the Settings or uninstall screens show "MAAR Shield" while protection is on, so the app cannot be removed on impulse. It does not read, store or transmit any screen content.
  • Device admin: used only to prevent uninstalling while protection is on.

Data

MAAR Shield collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Domain names that are not blocked are resolved through Cloudflare's family DNS (1.1.1.3), which is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy. Settings are stored only on your device.
